# Wedding Invitation — Delivery Outcomes

## Decorated envelope landing experience
- [x] Show a closed, decorated wedding envelope with a field asking the visitor to enter their name: “Enter your name”.
- [x] Keep the envelope and invitation card as the visual focus, with delicate floral details and tasteful decorative accents.

## Name personalization and reveal
- [x] Let guests type their name in their preferred style.
- [x] Preserve the guest’s exact entered capitalization, spacing, and styling in the warm personal greeting.
- [x] When guests click “Open your invitation,” animate the envelope opening and reveal the invitation.

## Invitation copy and editable details
- [x] Display “Abdullah and Rahma joyfully invite you to celebrate their wedding” with a personal “Dear [Guest Name],” greeting and the requested welcoming copy.
- [x] Keep the venue name, wedding date, and venue location easy to update in `src/main.js` through the `EVENT_DETAILS` object.
- [x] Use the provided wedding date `12 / 12` and do not invent the venue name, year, time, or location.

## Visual system and responsive layout
- [x] Use a refined palette of ivory, soft gold, and blush pink.
- [x] Pair an elegant script/display treatment with highly readable invitation-detail typography.
- [x] Keep the experience clean, readable, and easy to use on both phones and desktop computers.
- [x] Add smooth, subtle animations for envelope opening and invitation reveal, with reduced-motion support.

## Background music
- [x] Include gentle instrumental wedding background music with autoplay attempted on entry.
- [x] Start playback after the first visitor interaction if browser autoplay restrictions block entry playback.
- [x] Include a clear pause/resume music toggle.
